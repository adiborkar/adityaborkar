Current Works / Interactive

Guitar Daisies

Play a guitar in front of your camera. Every pluck throws daisies out of the sound hole and off your fretting fingers, and they fall and take root along the bottom of the frame.

Your browser will ask for the camera and microphone. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. It all runs on this page.

Input level (pink line is the level a pluck has to beat)

Raise sensitivity if daisies rarely appear, lower it if they never stop.

View

Works best on a laptop in Chrome, Safari or Edge, with the guitar's sound hole in the lower half of the frame. Without a guitar, clapping or singing works too: the daisies then start from the middle of the picture.
