Current Works / Interactive

Harmoniser

Play or sing one note and four more join it. Pick the chord with the number keys, or by where you look: left, right, up and down each call a different harmony, and looking straight ahead leaves you on your own.

Put headphones on first, or the microphone will hear the harmonies and howl. Your browser will ask for the camera and microphone. Nothing is recorded or sent anywhere. It all runs on this page.

Eye triggers

Press Calibrate eyes once and follow the prompts. It takes about 20 seconds and is remembered on this browser.

What each look plays

Works best on a laptop in Chrome or Edge, with headphones and one note at a time: sax, flute, voice, anything with a clear pitch. The SIGNAL meter shows how loud you are and the TUNE meter shows how many cents sharp or flat.

This is the browser version of a TouchDesigner patch I play saxophone into.
